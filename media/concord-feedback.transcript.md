# Testing Concord: how to take part and give feedback

## Welcome (0:00)

Hi, and thank you for helping us test Concord.

In about three minutes I'll show you how the alpha works, and the easiest ways to tell us what you think.

## The welcome page (0:10)

Everything starts on this page. It explains what Concord is for, and it has a short tour of the whole platform if you'd like the bigger picture.

Taking part has four steps. Pick a role, try a few tasks, let the simulated participants act, and then tell us what you think. There's no account to create.

When you're ready, press Start the guided walkthrough.

## Pick a role (0:35)

The walkthrough saves your place in this browser, so you can stop and come back whenever you like.

First, choose a role. You could be the steward who looks after a standard, a domain expert, a voting member, someone from the ODI, or a developer building on it. Here, I'll be a voting member.

Each role has a few people to choose from. You play that person, and everyone else around you is simulated.

## Try the tasks (1:02)

Next, the walkthrough suggests a few things to try in that role. Press Go to open the right page, and tick each task off when you're done.

If you'd rather explore on your own, keep the guide open. It sits in the corner of every page, so you won't lose your place.

## Let the others act (1:20)

Real governance takes days, or weeks. So press Simulate the others, and the other participants comment, review and vote.

Then move the clock forward a day or a week, so reviews close and votes are counted.

The demo is shared with the other testers, so you may see their work too. Please don't type in anything confidential.

## Tell us what you think (1:44)

When you've seen enough, there are two ways to give feedback.

Guided feedback takes about eight minutes. An assistant asks a short set of questions about each part of Concord, including how you'd want its decisions to be made.

Or just tell us something. A bug, an idea, or something that confused you. Type it, or tap the microphone and talk.

The assistant may ask a follow-up question or two. Say as much or as little as you like.

When you're done, press Submit, and it goes straight to the team building Concord.

## Any time (2:22)

You don't have to finish the walkthrough first. Give feedback is at the top of every page, so tell us the moment something surprises you, good or bad.

## Thank you (2:32)

Thanks again for your time. Honest reactions help us most, so please don't hold back.
